# VendorPulse: 2-Page Executive Summary
**ModelX 2.0 | Team Nexio | IEEE CIS IIT & University of Moratuwa**

---

### Page 1: Problem Space, User Persona, and Core Innovation
* **The Vulnerability**: Daily liquidity collapse in informal street markets due to compounded external shocks (rainfall + transit suspensions).
* **Target User**: Micro-Finance NGO Field Outreach Coordinator.
* **Paradigm Shift**: From retroactive default management to 48–72h proactive financial shielding.
* **System Core**: Algorithmic extraction of compounded risk from multi-modal municipal data streams.

---

### Page 2: System Architecture, Real Data Impact, and Performance
* **Pipeline Execution**: Live weather + transit anomalies + NLP news parsing.
* **Risk Engine**: Quantile loss probabilistic forecasting exceeding Naïve & ARIMA baselines (FPR < 20%).
* **Field Action**: Dark-mode field dashboard enabling one-click bridge capital authorization to ground officers.
