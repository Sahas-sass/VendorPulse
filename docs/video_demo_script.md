# VendorPulse: 3-Minute System Demonstration Script

**Target Duration:** 180 seconds (3:00 minutes)  
**Presenter:** Sahas Abeygunarathne  
**Theme:** Real data early warning in action (Colombo, Sri Lanka)

---

### [0:00 - 0:40] Part 1: The Predatory Debt Trap & The Problem
- **Visual:** Urban street vendors in Pettah market, market stalls, rain clouds gathering.
- **Narration:** Explain the dilemma of daily liquidity — today's earnings buy tomorrow's inventory. An acute disruption forces vendors to borrow at 10-20% weekly rates.
- **The Gap:** NGOs arrive too late, after vendors default.

### [0:40 - 1:25] Part 2: VendorPulse Ingestion & Intelligence
- **Visual:** Terminal running live pipeline / backend logging real OpenWeatherMap and Colombo transit/news alerts.
- **Narration:** Show multi-modal data streams coming together: weather storm front + Bus Route 138 suspension + flooded drainage. The ML engine calculates a 90% liquidity failure probability 48 hours prior.

### [1:25 - 2:25] Part 3: The NGO Coordinator Experience (Live UI Walkthrough)
- **Visual:** Dark-mode Threat Map in action.
- **Narration:**
  - Coordinator views the red critical zone over Pettah Block P-04.
  - Inspects impact forecast: 142 registered vendors, Rs 2.5M capital requirement.
  - Reviews live risk drivers.
  - Clicks **"Authorize Bridge Deployment"**.
  - Shows real-time backend audit logging and field dispatch confirmation.

### [2:25 - 3:00] Part 4: Empirical Evaluation & Real-World Impact
- **Visual:** Model evaluation charts: comparison against Naïve & ARIMA baselines, FPR < 20%, Quantile loss.
- **Narration:** Summarize how proactive shielding prevents hundreds of vendors from falling into debt cycles. Closing statement.
